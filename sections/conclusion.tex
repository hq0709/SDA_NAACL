\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Importance judgments in LLM memory admission favor passage words, discarding useful implied concepts the model recognizes. Statedness-cued wording and statedness rules shift the preference between groups. SDA trains that judgment on the model's own signals, removing the strong form and narrowing the gap between conditions A and B while improving admission of stated and implied useful concepts. Evaluating admission requires statedness-balanced candidate pools, with retention and answer accuracy reported separately for stated and implied episodes.

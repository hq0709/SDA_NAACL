\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Importance judgments exhibit the Statedness Bias: they favor passage words despite recognizing implied useful concepts. On Qwen3-8B, statedness-cued wording reverses this preference, trading performance between stated and implied episodes. Admission evaluation should control statedness and report useful-concept retention and answer accuracy separately for both groups (Table~\ref{tab:splitfull}). SDA trains importance judgments on the model's signals through an admission-only adapter. It removes the bias's strong form and narrows the A-B gap from 54.1 to 25.0 points, improving stated and implied retention and TwoHopFact answer accuracy without training on it.

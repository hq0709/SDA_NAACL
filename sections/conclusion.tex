\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}\label{sec:implications}

Importance judgments favor stated candidates even when the useful concept is unstated. SDA trains a LoRA adapter through the importance prompt on labels from the frozen model's workspace readout and output likelihood. It improves answer accuracy over each model's importance report on the statedness-balanced pool; the workspace readout supports unstated useful-concept retention. vLLM serves Qwen3-8B at 242 candidates per second, within 0.3 points of offline answer accuracy. Admission evaluation should compare policies on identical episodes with a fixed reader and budget, balance useful-concept statedness, include unstated distractors, and report within-episode ranking, retention split by statedness and answer accuracy.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Importance judgments favor candidates the passage states, even when
the useful concept is unstated.
SDA trains a LoRA adapter, through the model's importance prompt, on
labels that the frozen model derives from its workspace readout and
output likelihood. It improves answer accuracy over each model's
importance report on the statedness-balanced pool, and the workspace
readout supports retention of unstated useful concepts.
Served through vLLM, the Qwen3-8B adapter stays within 0.3 points of its offline
answer accuracy at 242 candidates per second.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Importance judgments exhibit the Statedness Bias: they favor passage words despite recognizing implied useful concepts. On Qwen3-8B, statedness-cued wording reverses this preference, trading performance between stated and implied episodes. Admission evaluation should control statedness and report useful-concept retention and answer accuracy separately for both groups (Table~\ref{tab:splitfull}). SDA trains the importance judgment on the model's own signals through an adapter used only for admission. It removes the strong form of the Statedness Bias and narrows the A-B gap from 54.1 to 25.0 points, while improving admission of stated and implied useful concepts.

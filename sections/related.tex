\section{Background and Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Memory Admission}\label{sec:problem}

Memory admission selects the information available to a model after a passage is removed. Given passage $x$ and ordered candidates $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$, memory admission stores $K\subseteq C$, $|K|\leq k$, before future question $q$ and answer $a$ are known; the frozen reader answers $q$ from $K$ alone. All policies receive the same candidates and budget, so comparisons isolate the admission decision. Importance judgments store the top $k$ by score.

The importance prompt $u(c,x)$ asks whether $c$ is important for possible future questions, requesting a single \emph{yes} or \emph{no} (App.~\ref{app:prompts}). Let $Y$ and $N$ contain distinct first tokens of the case and leading-space forms of \emph{yes} and \emph{no}:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
s_\theta(c;x)={}&\log\!\sum_{t\in Y}p_\theta(t\mid u(c,x))\\
&-\log\!\sum_{t\in N}p_\theta(t\mid u(c,x)),\\
V_\theta(c;x)={}&\sigma\!\left(s_\theta(c;x)\right).
\end{split}
\label{eq:score}
\end{equation}
The untouched model's $V_\theta$ is the importance report. The 1--10 importance rating and published admission criteria also rank future usefulness to determine whether passage words displace useful concepts.

\subsection{Stated and Implied Concepts}\label{sec:statedness}

Define statedness $S(c,x)=1$ when a case-insensitive stem match finds candidate $c$ in passage $x$, and $S(c,x)=0$ otherwise. Each episode's useful concept $b$ links the passage to the future answer; other candidates are distractors. An unstated $b$ is \emph{implied}. Statedness and usefulness are separate properties: a passage word is not necessarily useful, and leaving a concept unstated does not reduce its usefulness. In Figure~\ref{fig:episode}, \texttt{mesopotamia} is implied and useful, whereas \texttt{silt} is a stated distractor. Naming \texttt{mesopotamia} changes statedness without changing usefulness.

For episode $i$, let $D_i^d$ contain distractors with statedness $d$, and $\mathcal E_{r,d}$ contain episodes with $S(b_i,x_i)=r$ and $D_i^d\neq\varnothing$. Write $s_i(c)=s(c;x_i)$. The win rate averages over these episodes and their distractors:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathrm{WR}_{r,d}(s)={}&\frac{1}{|\mathcal E_{r,d}|}
\sum_{i\in\mathcal E_{r,d}}\frac{1}{|D_i^d|}\\
&\quad\cdot\sum_{c\in D_i^d}\phi(s_i(b_i),s_i(c)),\\
\phi(u,v)={}&\mathbf{1}\{u>v\}+\tfrac12\mathbf{1}\{u=v\}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:winrate}
\end{equation}
A judgment exhibits the \textbf{Statedness Bias} when $\mathrm{WR}_{0,1}<\mathrm{WR}_{0,0}$: implied useful concepts win less often against stated than against unstated distractors. The \textbf{strong form}, $\mathrm{WR}_{0,1}<1/2$, means the judgment prefers a stated distractor to the useful concept. An unbiased judgment gives the same win rate whatever the distractor's statedness, holding useful-concept statedness fixed. Statedness AUC is the probability that a stated candidate outranks an unstated one, counting ties half.

\subsection{Related Work}\label{sec:relwork}

\paragraph{Memory systems and admission.}
Generative Agents uses importance alongside recency and relevance \citep{park2023generative}; MemoryBank, MemGPT, Mem0, and A-MEM organize persistent memory \citep{zhong2023memorybank,packer2023memgpt,mem0,amem2025}; LoCoMo and LongMemEval evaluate conversational memory \citep{maharana2024locomo,wu2025longmemeval}, while MemCon and AgeMem learn memory operations \citep{memcon2026,agemem2026}; \citet{hu2025memorysurvey} surveys these systems. Admission approaches use contextual support \citep{consistencygate2026,amac2026}, evidence retention \citep{valuemodel2026}, memory interventions for known requests \citep{cmi2026}, and prediction error \citep{nemori2026}.

\paragraph{Verbal reports and internal knowledge.}
Internal states predict correctness beyond expressed confidence \citep{kadavath2022know,azaria2023internal,orgad2024llmsknow}, verbal reports omit reasoning influences and salience \citep{turpin2023cot,trienes2025salience}, and introspection training teaches models to predict their behavior \citep{binder2024introspection}. We test whether importance judgments reject implied concepts the model recognizes.

\paragraph{Readouts of internal states.}
The logit lens projects hidden states into token predictions \citep{nostalgebraist2020logitlens}; related approaches include tuned lenses, Future Lens, Patchscopes \citep{belrose2023tuned,pal2023futurelens,ghandeharioun2024patchscopes}, and Jacobian-based lenses for verbalizable concepts \citep{anthropic2026workspace}. We use the training-free logit lens for diagnosis (\S\ref{sec:knowledge}) and SDA's training labels.

\paragraph{Distillation and weak supervision.}
Distillation transfers teacher predictions to a student \citep{hinton2015distilling,furlanello2018bornagain}; TMEM distills experience into low-rank weights \citep{tmem2026}, Agent Memory Distillation transfers larger-agent trajectories into smaller-agent memory \citep{amd2026}, and strong students trained on weaker labels can exceed their supervisors \citep{burns2023weak}. No prior work separates statedness from usefulness in admission or trains importance judgments on the model's own signals; this paper does both.

\section{Background and Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Memory Admission}\label{sec:problem}

Given passage $x$ and ordered candidates $C=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$, admission stores $K\subseteq C$, $|K|\leq k$, before future question $q$ and answer $a$ are known. After passage removal, the frozen reader answers $q$ from $K$ and its knowledge. Policies share candidates, budgets and reader, isolating admission.

The importance prompt $u(c,x)$ asks whether $c$ is important for possible future questions, based only on the passage, requesting a single \emph{yes} or \emph{no} (App.~\ref{app:prompts}). Let $Y,N$ contain distinct first tokens of bare and leading-space \emph{yes}/\emph{no} forms in lowercase, initial-capital and uppercase:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
s_\theta(c;x)={}&\log\!\sum_{t\in Y}p_\theta(t\mid u(c,x))\\
&-\log\!\sum_{t\in N}p_\theta(t\mid u(c,x)),\\
V_\theta(c;x)={}&\sigma\!\left(s_\theta(c;x)\right).
\end{split}
\label{eq:score}
\end{equation}
Here $p_\theta$ is the next-token distribution. The sigmoid preserves ordering and gives the \emph{yes} share of the combined \emph{yes}/\emph{no} probability. The importance report stores the top $k$ under $V_\theta$ without an adapter.

\subsection{Stated and Implied Concepts}\label{sec:statedness}

Statedness $S(c,x)=1$ when a case-insensitive stem match finds $c$ in $x$, and $0$ otherwise. Stated candidates are passage words. Each episode's useful concept $b$ links passage to answer; other candidates are distractors. An unstated $b$ is \emph{implied}. Naming $b$ changes statedness without changing usefulness.

Let $D_i^d$ contain episode $i$'s distractors with statedness $d$, and $\mathcal E_{r,d}$ contain episodes with $S(b_i,x_i)=r$ and $D_i^d\neq\varnothing$. For policy score $s_i(c)=s(c;x_i)$, win rate pools within-episode comparisons:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathrm{WR}_{r,d}(s)={}&\frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal E_{r,d}}\sum_{c\in D_i^d}\phi(s_i(b_i),s_i(c))}{\sum_{i\in\mathcal E_{r,d}}|D_i^d|},\\
\phi(u,v)={}&\mathbf{1}\{u>v\}+\tfrac12\mathbf{1}\{u=v\}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:winrate}
\end{equation}
Ties receive half credit. The \textbf{Statedness Bias}, $\mathrm{WR}_{0,1}<\mathrm{WR}_{0,0}$, means implied useful concepts win less often against stated than unstated distractors. Its \textbf{strong form}, $\mathrm{WR}_{0,1}<1/2$, prefers stated distractors to useful concepts. An unbiased judgment has equal win rates across distractor statedness, holding useful-concept statedness fixed. Statedness AUC measures \emph{statedness preference}: the probability a stated candidate outranks an unstated one, counting ties half.

\subsection{Related Work}\label{sec:relwork}

\paragraph{Memory systems and admission.}
Systems score importance \citep{park2023generative}, organize memory \citep{zhong2023memorybank,packer2023memgpt,mem0,amem2025}, and learn memory operations \citep{memcon2026,agemem2026}. Conversational benchmarks \citep{maharana2024locomo,wu2025longmemeval} cover these systems, surveyed by \citet{hu2025memorysurvey}. Admission criteria use support \citep{consistencygate2026,amac2026}, evidence retention \citep{valuemodel2026}, interventions \citep{cmi2026}, and prediction error \citep{nemori2026}; we test implied-concept admission.

\paragraph{Verbal reports and internal knowledge.}
Internal states predict correctness beyond confidence \citep{kadavath2022know,azaria2023internal,orgad2024llmsknow}; reports omit reasoning influences and salience \citep{turpin2023cot,trienes2025salience}. Introspection training predicts model behavior \citep{binder2024introspection}. We compare importance reports with internal signals.

\paragraph{Readouts of internal states.}
We use the logit lens \citep{nostalgebraist2020logitlens}; alternatives include tuned lenses, Future Lens, Patchscopes \citep{belrose2023tuned,pal2023futurelens,ghandeharioun2024patchscopes}, and Jacobian-based lenses \citep{anthropic2026workspace}.

\paragraph{Distillation and weak supervision.}
Distillation transfers predictions \citep{hinton2015distilling,furlanello2018bornagain}, experience into weights \citep{tmem2026}, and agent trajectories into memory \citep{amd2026}. Students can exceed weaker supervisors \citep{burns2023weak}. SDA trains importance judgments on the model's own signals while separating statedness from usefulness.

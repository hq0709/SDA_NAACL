SDA trains the importance judgment through the importance prompt, on labels from internal signals that lack the judgment's statedness preference (\S\ref{sec:knowledge}). A LoRA adapter serves only admission (Figure~\ref{fig:sda}; Algorithm~\ref{alg:sda} in App.~\ref{app:training}).

\paragraph{Teacher signals.}
At the final passage position, $z_\ell(x)=W_U\,\mathrm{LN}(h_\ell(x))$ for $1\leq\ell\leq L$, where $h_\ell$ is the residual state after layer $\ell$ for $\ell<L$ and $h_L$ is the final normalized state the model returns. The logit-lens readout takes the peak reciprocal rank:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathrm{rank}_\ell(t)&=1+|\{v:z_{\ell,v}>z_{\ell,t}\}|,\\
R(c;x)&=\max_{\substack{1\leq\ell\leq L\\t\in F(c)}}\frac{1}{\mathrm{rank}_\ell(t)}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:wrr}
\end{equation}
$F(c)$ contains distinct first tokens of the bare and leading-space forms of $c$, in original and capitalized case. One forward pass serves all candidates. Ranks are compared only within an episode, so tokens leading every layer's ranking, such as articles, shift all candidates alike. Output likelihood is $\mathrm{LL}(c;x)=\log p(c\mid x)/|c|$, with token length $|c|$.

\paragraph{Labels and distillation.}
Within-episode mid-rank percentiles, with ties assigned their mean rank, give
\begin{equation}
T(c;x)=\tfrac12\bigl(P_R(c;x)+P_{\mathrm{LL}}(c;x)\bigr).
\label{eq:teacher}
\end{equation}
The top two candidates receive $\tilde y=1$ (\emph{yes}), the rest $\tilde y=0$ (\emph{no}); candidate order breaks ties. With base weights frozen, rank-16 LoRA \citep{hu2021lora} on every attention and feed-forward projection learns these labels through the importance prompt:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\mathcal L(\theta)={}&-\frac{1}{|\mathcal D|}\sum_{(x,c)\in\mathcal D}\\
&\quad\cdot\log p_\theta\!\left(\tau(\tilde y(c;x))\mid u(c,x)\right).
\end{split}
\label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}
$\mathcal D$ contains passage--candidate pairs; $\tau$ maps labels to target tokens. Probabilities are normalized over the vocabulary; prompt tokens are masked. Training runs two epochs, retaining the earliest checkpoint with highest validation within-episode AUC. Gold utility labels serve only validation (App.~\ref{app:training}). Results average three training runs. SDA ranks by $s_\theta$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:score}) with the adapter enabled and stores the top $k$.

\paragraph{Why the student can exceed its teacher.}
The adapter starts from the model's importance judgment, which already ranks implied useful concepts well when no stated distractor competes (condition B). The labels target its preference for passage words. A strong student trained on weaker labels can exceed its teacher \citep{burns2023weak}; Section~\ref{sec:learns} tests this account.

\paragraph{Serving.}
SDA uses one yes/no forward pass per candidate with passage prefix caching \citep{kwon2023pagedattention}.

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

An LLM agent must choose what to remember before it knows the next question, and the useful concept is often an inference that the passage never states. A landmark can identify a city without naming it; remembering that city preserves the link to a later question about the local language (Figure~\ref{fig:episode}).

\input{figures/fig_sda_teaser}

Memory systems make this choice through importance judgments: the yes/no importance report, the 1--10 importance rating \citep{park2023generative}, or published criteria built on context support \citep{amac2026,consistencygate2026}. These judgments favor candidates the passage states. Among unstated candidates, the importance report's pooled area under the ROC curve (AUC) is 0.880--0.897 across six models. On the original pool, where the useful concept is usually the only unstated candidate, it falls below chance (0.322--0.427).

The workspace readout ranks the useful concept above chance on the same original pool. It uses the logit lens \citep{nostalgebraist2020logitlens} to score concepts in the model's workspace \citep{anthropic2026workspace}. Naming the useful concept raises its AUC far more than the importance report's (Figure~\ref{fig:hero}); the model's intermediate layers encode what its importance report does not express.

Self-Distilled Admission (SDA) uses the frozen model as its own teacher, ranking each episode's candidates by workspace readout and output likelihood and labeling the top two for admission. A LoRA adapter \citep{hu2021lora} learns these labels through the importance prompt and is used only for admission requests (Figure~\ref{fig:sda}).

On the statedness-balanced pool, SDA exceeds each model's importance report and 1--10 importance rating in all 18 comparisons across six models and three budgets (Figure~\ref{fig:results}). Qwen3-8B gains 10.7 answer accuracy points over its importance report and 5.2 over its 1--10 importance rating at two stored concepts. ConsistencyGate, A-MAC and Nemori fall below the untrained importance report; SDA exceeds them by 14.1--27.8 points (Table~\ref{tab:balancedbaselines}). Removing the workspace readout from the teacher lowers unstated useful-concept retention in each of five models. Served through vLLM, the Qwen3-8B adapter scores 242 candidates per second on the original pool within 0.3 points of its offline answer accuracy (\S\ref{sec:deployment}).

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Statedness preference:} Importance judgments favor stated candidates, while the workspace readout ranks unstated useful concepts above chance (\S\ref{sec:gap}).
    \item \textbf{Self-Distilled Admission:} SDA distills the workspace readout and output likelihood into a LoRA adapter queried through the model's importance prompt and used only for admission (\S\ref{sec:method}).
    \item \textbf{Answer accuracy gains:} SDA improves answer accuracy over the importance report, the 1--10 importance rating and published criteria across six models, three budgets and five candidate pools, with ablations that isolate each teacher signal (\S\ref{sec:results}).
\end{itemize}

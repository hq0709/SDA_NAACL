\section{Experimental Settings}\label{sec:settings}\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Models}

We evaluate Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, Qwen3-32B, Qwen2.5-7B (Instruct), OLMo-2-7B (1124 Instruct), and Mistral-7B (Instruct v0.3) \citep{qwen3,qwen2025,olmo2,jiang2023mistral}, with Qwen3 thinking disabled, greedy decoding, and each model's frozen reader without an adapter shared across policies at $k=1,2,3$.

\subsection{Admission Policies}\label{sec:baselines}

The importance report ranks by the unadapted model's $V_\theta$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:score}); the 1--10 importance rating changes the response format \citep{park2023generative}. On Qwen3-8B, we evaluate sampled and single-pass ConsistencyGate, A-MAC utility, and prediction error \citep{consistencygate2026,amac2026,nemori2026}; prediction error is candidate surprisal under the passage (App.~\ref{app:publishedbaselines}).

Implied-content wording asks the model to consider what the passage implies as well as states. Statedness-cued wording asks whether storing a concept helps even if the passage never mentions it (Qwen3-8B; App.~\ref{app:wording}).

Unstated-first prioritizes unstated candidates; unstated-first with report ordering retains the report's order within each statedness group. SDA uses the trained judgment (\S\ref{sec:sda}). Gold-concept selection ranks the useful concept first as a reference. App.~\ref{app:baselines} gives policy details.

\subsection{Metrics}

Win rate measures useful-concept ranking in each statedness condition (Eq.~\eqref{eq:winrate}). Within-episode AUC uses the same pairwise comparisons over all distractors. Pooled useful-concept AUC compares useful concepts with distractors across episodes.

Statedness AUC measures the probability that a stated candidate outranks an unstated one: $1/2$ indicates no preference; higher values favor passage words. Statedness correlation is the Pearson correlation between candidate scores and binary statedness, pooled over candidates.

Useful-concept retention $B_k=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathbf{1}\{b_i\in K_i\}$ measures the share of $n$ episodes storing the useful concept, reported separately for stated and implied episodes (Table~\ref{tab:splitfull}). Answer accuracy is the share of frozen reader answers accepted by a blind semantic judge (App.~\ref{app:judge}). Win rate, retention, and accuracy are percentages; AUC uses its probability scale (Table~\ref{tab:splitfull}).

Bold marks the best value in each comparison except in Table~\ref{tab:grid}, which shades win rates below 50\%; gold-concept selection is a reference and is not marked.

\section{Reducing the Statedness Bias}\label{sec:results}\label{sec:recall}

\subsection{Useful Concepts Outrank Stated Distractors}\label{sec:correct}

\myparagraph{Win rates.}
SDA raises condition A's mean win rate from 38.3\% to 67.5\%, removing the strong form in every model (58.9--77.2\%); condition C rises from 50.6\% to 79.5\%, improving discrimination among passage words. Conditions B and D remain high at 92.5\% and 98.9\% (Table~\ref{tab:grid}). The gap between conditions A and B narrows from 54.1 to 25.0 points.

\myparagraph{Retention and accuracy.}
On the statedness-balanced pool at $k=2$, six-model mean retention rises from 45.3\% to 80.3\% on stated episodes and 33.4\% to 63.8\% on implied episodes (Table~\ref{tab:split}). In every model, SDA exceeds the importance report, the 1--10 importance rating, unstated-first with report ordering, unstated-first, and the reversed importance report in both groups' answer accuracy. Qwen3-8B overall accuracy reaches 53.7\% versus 43.0\%, 48.5\%, and 39.1\% for the importance report, rating, and unstated-first with report ordering. SDA exceeds A-MAC utility by 14.0 points, both ConsistencyGate forms by 18.7, and prediction error by 27.8.

\myparagraph{Budgets and pools.}
SDA exceeds the importance report and rating in all 18 model-budget comparisons on the statedness-balanced pool (App.~\ref{app:balancedresults}). Against the importance report, it wins all 18 on the original pool (mean gain: 9.6 points at $k=2$), all 18 on the pool with unstated distractors, and 17 of 18 on the naming-balanced pool; the remaining comparison trails by 0.1 points. Qwen3-8B gains 11.0 points on the enlarged pool at $k=2$ and exceeds the importance report on every development set and the held-out set (App.~\ref{app:poolresults}; App.~\ref{app:heldout}).

\subsection{What SDA Learns}\label{sec:learns}\label{sec:ablations}

\myparagraph{Student and teacher.}
On Qwen3-8B's statedness-balanced pool, SDA's within-episode rank correlation is 0.76 with the importance report and 0.22 with the teacher; direct teacher use yields 44.9\% answer accuracy at $k=2$, 8.8 points below SDA. Where their top candidates differ, SDA selects the useful concept in 93 episodes versus the teacher's 85 on the statedness-balanced pool, and 119 versus 37 on the original pool. SDA preserves useful ranking while reducing preference for passage words.

\myparagraph{Teacher ablations.}
On the statedness-balanced pool, removing the logit-lens readout lowers implied retention in all five ablated models (all except Mistral-7B) at every budget: mean losses are 6.6, 4.5, and 2.5 points at $k=1,2,3$. Removing output likelihood lowers stated retention by 2.2, 1.9, and 1.8 points, respectively. Passage replacement on Qwen3-8B's original pool at $k=1$ lowers SDA retention by 20.9 points versus 2.9 for the student trained without the readout, showing that the readout makes admission passage-dependent.

\myparagraph{Knowledge and capabilities.}
Across tested Qwen3 scales, adapter-enabled readout AUC changes by at most 0.0046; enabling the adapter on every token changes four capability scores by at most 0.3 points (App.~\ref{app:capability}).

\myparagraph{Deployment.}\label{sec:deployment}

With prefix caching, vLLM on one A100-80GB serves 242 candidates per second (4.1\,ms each), versus the importance report's 290 (3.4\,ms), over the original pool's 1,607 candidates. Served Qwen3-8B answer accuracy is 50.1\%, within 0.3 points of offline, with 91.7\% agreement in the top two stored concepts (App.~\ref{app:timing}).
